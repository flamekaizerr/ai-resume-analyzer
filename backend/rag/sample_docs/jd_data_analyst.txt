Job Title: Data Analyst

About the role
Join our analytics team to turn sales data into reports and insights for business teams.

Requirements
- Strong SQL skills
- Python with pandas for data cleaning
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Basic statistics and A/B testing
- Good communication with non technical stakeholders

Responsibilities
- Build weekly sales dashboards
- Clean and join data from multiple sources
- Present findings to managers
